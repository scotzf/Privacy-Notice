Penny Pocket Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

In short: Penny Pocket keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We do not have accounts, servers or analytics, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

What stays on your phone

The name you type, your pockets, entries, limits, goals and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted. We cannot recover them for you.

What the app does over the internet

The app works fully offline. Only if you tap Connect on the Insights screen, it downloads two kinds of public information directly from their websites: (1) news feeds (RSS) from BusinessWorld, Manila Times, GMA Network, Philstar, Abante and Rappler, to show price headlines; and (2) exchange rates from exchangerate-api.com. Like any website visit, those sites can see your device's IP address and the type of device. We send no personal information, no name and none of your financial entries. The headlines and rates are saved on your phone so they can be read offline. Tapping a headline opens the original article in your browser, which is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

Features that use your phone

  • App lock: uses Android's own fingerprint, face or PIN screen. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Daily reminder: an optional notification created on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Backup and restore: saves a file to a place you choose and reads a file you choose. The app does not upload it. Keep the file safe, because it contains your entries.
  • Contact: the Contact button opens your email app to write to the developer. An email is only sent if you send it.

Permissions

Internet (for the optional Connect), Biometrics (for the optional lock), Notifications (for the optional reminder) and Run at startup (to restore the reminder after your phone restarts).

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

Children

Penny Pocket is a general budgeting tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
