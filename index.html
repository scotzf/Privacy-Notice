Penny Pocket Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

In short: Penny Pocket keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We do not have accounts, servers or analytics, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

What stays on your phone

The name you type, your pockets, entries, limits, goals and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted. We cannot recover them for you.

What the app does over the internet

The app works fully offline. Only if you tap Connect on the Insights screen, it downloads public news feeds (RSS) directly from these news websites to show price headlines: BusinessWorld, Manila Times, GMA Network, Philstar and Rappler. Like any website visit, those sites can see your device's IP address and the type of device. We send no personal information, no name and none of your financial entries. The headlines are saved on your phone so they can be read offline. Tapping a headline opens the original article in your browser, which is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app asks only for internet access, used only as described above.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

Children

Penny Pocket is a general budgeting tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
